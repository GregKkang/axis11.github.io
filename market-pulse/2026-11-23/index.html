How the market response compared with historical patterns

The S&P 500 returned +4.73% against a model estimate of +0.39%. The gap was +4.34 percentage points. The extracted leadership and demand news mapped to a positive return, but the model captured only a small part of the actual advance. The close was above the upper 95% historical error band. Only one eligible calibration session had an absolute log-return error at least this large. A fixed linear mapping can miss a concentrated leadership shock, its interaction with earlier concerns and the trading dynamics surrounding it. The residual does not establish that the rally was excessive.

An absolute log-return error at least this large occurred on 0.45% of the same eligible calibration sample. This compares same-day outcomes with historical news–return relationships; it is not a future-return forecast or a causal fair-value estimate.

CNBC recap and supporting context

CNBC centered its closing account on Nvidia’s decision to bring Jensen Huang back as CEO, with President Trump saying he had pressed the board to do so. The rebound was broad across AI-related names. The recap also retained a counterweight: strong reported demand across the supply chain did not remove financing, geopolitical and durability concerns.

Text used by the model

The briefing author performed generative extraction and same-assistant source checks. This differs from the original Claude extractor used for training and is not independent review. The complete featured closing narrative was used; rolling updates were outside the fixed input scope. The input was locked before inference, and realized prices, returns, positioning and market-response commentary were removed. Reported opinions retain their attribution. Earlier events are background, not newly announced events.
