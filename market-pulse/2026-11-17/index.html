How the market response compared with historical patterns

The S&P 500 returned -1.03% against a model estimate of +0.11%. The gap was -1.14 percentage points. The model estimated a small gain from the extracted facts while the S&P 500 declined. The actual return fell below the 80% band but remained inside the 95% band. Absolute errors at least this large occurred on about one in seven eligible days. The gap indicates that this news representation did not reconstruct the session’s direction or magnitude. It does not isolate a causal contribution from the outage or Nvidia.

On 13.43% of eligible evaluation days, the model’s absolute log-return error was at least this large. The gap measures what the model missed at the index level. It does not establish that the market overreacted or underreacted to a particular event.

CNBC recap and supporting context

CNBC described another decline ahead of Nvidia earnings, alongside a report of disruption to an NSA network. The report also covered Huang’s willingness to return and neocloud financing concerns.

FRED confirmed all three November 16 comparison closes. The November 17 closes agree across CNBC’s closing narrative and closing update. The retrieved FRED pages did not yet show the November 16 observations. CNBC closing recap · FRED S&P 500 · FRED Dow · FRED Nasdaq

CNBC also carried the NSA’s subsequent account that routine maintenance caused temporary degradation while critical capabilities remained operational. The input retains the earlier attributed report and its qualification that there was no evidence of a cyberattack. The source timing limitation prevents establishing which version of the account was available at the regular close.

Text used by the model

The briefing author performed generative extraction and same-assistant checks against the complete closing narrative. This extractor differs from the original Claude extraction used to construct the training data. The input was locked before inference. Nvidia’s earnings remained a scheduled after-close release. The NSA’s later maintenance statement is discussed below but excluded from the input because its availability before 4 p.m. could not be established. Realized prices, returns, market positioning, and rolling liveblog entries were excluded.

CNN reported that a classified National Security Agency network had gone down, citing six people familiar with the matter. The report said there was no evidence of a cyberattack.
The technology infrastructure backdrop included a recent FAA ground stop, a Microsoft Outlook outage the previous week and issues with banking apps.
Nvidia's earnings report was scheduled for after Tuesday's regular-session close.
Jensen Huang said he would be willing to return as Nvidia CEO if its board considered that in shareholders' interests.
Baird strategist Ross Mayfield expected strong Nvidia results and guidance, while anticipating that leadership and export-control investigations would remain unresolved.
Steve Eisman warned that AI neocloud companies such as Iren and Nebius were exposed if Nvidia reduced its financing of the AI ecosystem.

CNBC displayed conflicting November 17 and November 18 update dates for the same recap. The first-publication timestamp and version available at the close could not be verified.

The fixed model uses local BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5 embeddings and Ridge alpha=1 fitted on 750 sessions in 2023–2025. The model hash was verified before inference.

Calibration uses 216 out-of-training 2026 sessions through November 10. The excluded sessions are November 11, November 12, November 13, November 16, November 17, the target day and four immediately preceding NYSE trading sessions. The frequency and bands use this same sample.

The 80% and 95% half-widths are the sorted absolute log-return errors at ranks ceil((216+1)×0.80)=174 and ceil((216+1)×0.95)=207. The half-widths are 0.0099470498 and 0.0181100313. Add and subtract each from the model log estimate, then convert the endpoints to simple returns using 100×(exp(x)−1). The bands describe historical errors in reconstructing same-day returns.
