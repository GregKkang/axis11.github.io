CNBC recap and supporting context

AI-related shares helped lift all three major U.S. indexes after Anthropic publicly filed for an IPO. Nvidia, Micron, Broadcom and AMD participated in the advance as investors reassessed the sector’s growth prospects.

The filing also disclosed a sharp increase in Anthropic’s revenue. Other financing headlines added to the focus on AI investment: Meta said it planned to raise $95 billion, while Elon Musk said SpaceX had completed a $40 billion debt deal for AI chips.

These announcements improved the tone, but did not settle questions about the sustainability or eventual returns of the investment boom. Investors were also approaching a busy earnings week and Wednesday’s Fed decision. CNBC reported that an unchanged policy-rate range was widely expected.
