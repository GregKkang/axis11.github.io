How the market response compared with historical patterns

The S&P 500 returned +0.36%, against a model estimate of +0.40%. The actual return was 0.03 percentage points below the estimate based on historical news relationships.

Taken together, the day’s extracted news was associated with a modest positive index return in the historical model, and the actual gain was close to that estimate. The absolute log-return error was smaller than on 96.1% of the 206 eligible evaluation days. This was a small aggregate gap rather than evidence of an unusually strong or weak market response.

That agreement has a narrower meaning than explaining every part of the session. The regression estimates one index-level return; it does not establish why semiconductor shares recovered, quantify Apple’s individual contribution, or show that any particular event was underreacted to or overreacted to. The positive estimate also does not mean that the model assigns a positive effect to each input.

The model remains fixed: 750 training sessions from 2023–2025, Ridge alpha=1, and unchanged local BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5 embeddings. No coefficients were refitted.

Error-band calibration for this briefing: 206 validated, out-of-training evaluation sessions in 2026 through October 27. The five excluded sessions are October 28, October 29, October 30, November 2 and November 3: the target session and its four immediately preceding trading sessions. No target-day or future residual enters calibration. The sample expands as eligible validated observations become available.

The absolute log-return errors are sorted. The 80% and 95% half-widths use ranks ceil((206+1)×0.80)=166 and ceil((206+1)×0.95)=197, giving 0.0097756092 and 0.0172323218 in log-return units. Each half-width is added to and subtracted from the model’s log estimate, then converted with 100×(exp(x)−1). The historical frequency above uses this same sample.

These are descriptive historical error bands, not fair-value ranges, causal estimates or guaranteed coverage intervals. The earlier six briefings retain their original calibration. Historical model and source notes.
