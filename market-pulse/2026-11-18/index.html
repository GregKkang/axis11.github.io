How the market response compared with historical patterns

The S&P 500 returned -0.23% against a model estimate of -0.07%. The gap was -0.16 percentage points. The S&P 500 and the model both showed small negative returns. The actual decline was about 0.16 percentage points larger, a relatively small gap against historical reconstruction errors. Both error bands contained the observed return. The combination of better Nvidia results, stronger guidance and unresolved AI financing and leadership questions mapped to a near-flat aggregate reading. The Dow’s larger decline and the Nasdaq’s gain show dispersion beyond this S&P-only regression.

On 83.41% of eligible evaluation days, the model’s absolute log-return error was at least this large. The gap measures what the model missed at the index level. It does not establish that the market overreacted or underreacted to a particular event.

CNBC recap and supporting context

CNBC reported a fifth consecutive S&P 500 decline, even as the Nasdaq advanced. Nvidia’s earnings beat and stronger guidance were set against continuing leadership uncertainty and concerns about AI capital spending and debt financing.

FRED confirmed all three November 17 comparison closes and the November 18 S&P 500 and Dow closes. The Nasdaq’s November 18 close agrees across CNBC’s narrative and closing update, while its retrieved FRED page still ended on November 17. CNBC closing recap · FRED S&P 500 · FRED Dow · FRED Nasdaq

Text used by the model

The briefing author performed generative extraction and same-assistant checks against the complete closing narrative. This extractor differs from the original Claude extraction used to construct the training data. The input was locked before inference. Nvidia’s Tuesday after-close earnings and earnings-call statements are prior information for Wednesday. Realized prices, returns, market positioning, and rolling liveblog entries were excluded.

Nvidia's third-quarter profit and revenue exceeded analyst estimates.
Nvidia raised its revenue guidance for the fiscal year ending January 2028, forecasting 75% year-over-year growth.
Nvidia board chair Harvey Jones said at the start of the earnings call that the board did not intend to reinstate Jensen Huang as CEO.
Great Valley Advisor Group strategist Eric Parnell said AI capital spending and debt financing remained fundamental challenges.
Anthropic was preparing an IPO later in the week. Sources cited by CNBC on Tuesday expected pricing above the proposed range.

The CNBC page displayed a November 19 update label later than retrieval, while its narrative and closes identify Wednesday, November 18. Its modification time and point-in-time version remain unverified.

The ancillary liveblog also covered Fed minutes and Waller’s policy remarks. They were outside the closing-narrative source scope used by the fixed model. This limits the coverage of today’s reading, even with a small residual. A close aggregate fit does not establish that every important market development was represented.

Calculation

Intercept b = -0.0006704786
Weighted embedding sum w·x = -0.0000698980
Estimated log return b+w·x = -0.0007403765
100 × (exp(b+w·x) − 1) = -0.074010%

The fixed model uses local BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5 embeddings and Ridge alpha=1 fitted on 750 sessions in 2023–2025. The model hash was verified before inference.

Calibration uses 217 out-of-training 2026 sessions through November 11. The excluded sessions are November 12, November 13, November 16, November 17, November 18, the target day and four immediately preceding NYSE trading sessions. The frequency and bands use this same sample.

The 80% and 95% half-widths are the sorted absolute log-return errors at ranks ceil((217+1)×0.80)=175 and ceil((217+1)×0.95)=208. The half-widths are 0.0101068082 and 0.0181100313. Add and subtract each from the model log estimate, then convert the endpoints to simple returns using 100×(exp(x)−1). The bands describe historical errors in reconstructing same-day returns.
