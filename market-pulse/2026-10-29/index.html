CNBC recap and supporting context

The Nasdaq fell more than the S&P 500 and Dow as investors digested large-company earnings. Alphabet dropped about 12% after missing adjusted earnings and revenue expectations, reporting negative free cash flow and maintaining substantial capital-spending plans.

Caterpillar’s results were more nuanced: overall revenue and earnings exceeded expectations, but construction-industry sales were slightly below estimates. Microsoft also beat earnings and revenue forecasts, helped by Azure growth. Separately, its capital-spending commitment was interpreted as pointing to delays at infrastructure partners, weighing on neocloud suppliers.

The September PCE report showed accelerating inflation, with the monthly reading matching expectations and the annual rate slightly below estimates. The previous day’s Fed hold remained part of the backdrop. The timing alone does not establish either release as the direct cause of the day’s decline.
