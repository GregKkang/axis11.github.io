CNBC recap and supporting context

U.S. stocks posted a third consecutive advance as the Fed held its policy-rate range at 3.75%–4%. The decision had been widely anticipated. Chair Kevin Warsh continued to emphasize price stability and described employment conditions as positive.

Company news offered a separate source of support. Oracle outlined a fiscal 2030 revenue target of $225 billion and limits on additional fundraising. SK Hynix’s record quarterly profit also featured in CNBC’s explanation of semiconductor strength.

The session should therefore be read as a combination of an expected policy decision and favorable company developments. Alphabet and Microsoft were due to report after the close; their eventual results were not part of the information available ahead of the regular-session finish.
