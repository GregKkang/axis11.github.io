How the market response compared with historical patterns

The S&P 500 returned -0.50% against a model estimate of +0.18%. The gap was -0.68 percentage points. The model’s estimate was slightly positive, while the S&P 500 declined. The close nevertheless remained inside the 80% historical error band: the discrepancy was not unusual relative to earlier evaluation errors. This mismatch is consistent with a text input containing both supportive AI-demand assessments and adverse policy or governance risks; the model does not identify separate causal contributions from each item.

An absolute log-return error at least this large occurred on 36.20% of the same eligible calibration sample. This compares same-day outcomes with historical news–return relationships; it is not a future-return forecast or a causal fair-value estimate.

CNBC recap and supporting context

CNBC described a partial reversal of Monday’s technology rebound, with Nvidia and Anthropic under pressure while the Dow advanced. Its closing narrative balanced corporate-governance concerns and planned U.S. tariff increases against UBS’s view that cloud usage and computing demand still supported AI investment. Text used by the model

The briefing author performed generative extraction and same-assistant source checks. This differs from the original Claude extractor used for training and is not independent review. The complete featured closing narrative was used; rolling updates were outside the fixed input scope. The input was locked before inference, and realized prices, returns, positioning and market-response commentary were removed. Reported opinions retain their attribution. Earlier events are background, not newly announced events.

The model remains fixed: local BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5 embeddings and Ridge alpha=1, fitted on 750 sessions in 2023–2025. Model and embedding asset hashes were verified; no refitting or paid API calls were used.

Calibration uses 221 out-of-training 2026 sessions through November 17. Excluded sessions: November 18, November 19, November 20, November 23, November 24. These are the target session and its four preceding NYSE sessions, not the last five available data rows. The extreme-error frequency uses the same calibration sample.

The absolute log-error order-statistic ranks are ceil((221+1)×0.80)=178 and ceil((221+1)×0.95)=211. Their half-widths are 0.0102657592 and 0.0181100313.
