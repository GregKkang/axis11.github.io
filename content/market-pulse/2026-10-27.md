## CNBC recap and supporting context

The Nasdaq outpaced the Dow as semiconductor shares extended their advance. CNBC highlighted $63 billion in financing announced by a group of banks and Blackstone for the purchase of Broadcom technology for Anthropic.

Company results provided a second source of support. JetBlue rose sharply despite a larger-than-expected quarterly loss after pointing to further margin expansion in the fourth quarter. UPS also advanced following better-than-expected results. The two examples underline the distinction between reported performance and forward guidance.

Iranian leaders discussed conditions for ending the conflict, while investors prepared for the Fed’s decision the following afternoon. A hold was expected; the more open question was whether officials would signal another increase before year-end.
