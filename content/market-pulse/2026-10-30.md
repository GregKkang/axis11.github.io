## CNBC recap and supporting context

The technology sell-off intensified, leaving the Nasdaq down 2.86% and the S&P 500 down 1.82%. Intel plunged almost 29% after issuing a weak outlook that pointed to softer PC demand. Amazon also fell after reporting earnings and revenue below expectations, negative free cash flow of $42.4 billion and an unchanged capital-spending forecast.

The pressure spread across semiconductor shares. Sandisk declined despite beating quarterly estimates and announcing a stock split, showing that favorable headline results did not guarantee a favorable share-price response.

CNBC also cited a Financial Times report that Anthropic might not secure an investment-grade rating after its debut. That added to questions about financing the AI buildout. Consumer staples and healthcare held up better, limiting the Dow’s decline. Month-end positioning and the approaching midterm elections were additional features of the session.
