An early rally faded, leaving the Nasdaq and S&P 500 lower even as the Dow advanced. Weakness in major technology stocks contrasted with gains in several industrial and consumer-related companies.

AI financing remained in focus. Marvell and Lumentum fell after announcing a $115 billion acquisition, while Oracle declined after outlining plans to issue $10 billion in stock and $10 billion in bonds for its AI buildout. Apple’s earnings were still due after the close.

Oil fell sharply amid hopes for renewed U.S.–Iran talks. Secretary of State Marco Rubio said Washington preferred a peaceful resolution, while Iranian comments left the door open to an agreement. Those developments did not prevent technology weakness from weighing on the main growth-oriented indexes. Investors also faced Tuesday’s midterm elections and the week’s jobs data.
